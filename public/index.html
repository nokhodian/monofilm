The problem

AI film tools are fast, and you cannot trust the result.

How most tools work

You type a prompt, wait, and get a finished clip. If a face drifts or a room changes between shots, you start again. Nobody decided anything, so nobody can say what to fix.

How Monofilm works

The AI makes one thing at a time and shows it. You decide. What you approve is kept, what you refuse is redone, and everything built on a change is marked out of date.

How it works

Three beats, repeated on every page.

  1. 1

    You tell it

    Write or paste a story, add notes, choose a look. Say what you want in your own words, or speak it.

  2. 2

    It makes something to review

    Muse Spark and Muse Image propose people, places, scenes and pictures. Nothing is final and nothing runs without you.

  3. 3

    You press one button to go on

    Approve it, or fix it first. Every version is kept, so you can always go back.

The seven steps

One guided path from a story to a finished pack.

The first five steps are decided once for the whole film. Scenes each get their own five pages. Colour tells you where you are.

Click anything, fix it

Point at what is off. It changes only that.

Click anything the AI made and a margin card opens. Say what is wrong, in a few taps or your own words, or press the microphone and speak it. Pin it on the picture. Keep what is good.

  • See what it will touch and what it will cost before anything runs.
  • Press Fix it, or leave a note for later.
  • Everything is versioned. Change something and what was built on it is marked out of date, not silently redone.

The Stage

Place people and cameras before a single picture is made.

Drag each person, object and camera on a floor plan, or open a 3D editor. The studio checks that everyone is in frame and nothing is hiding behind a wall.

That layout feeds the picture model, and SAM 3.1 later checks each picture against it and says what drifted.

Choose what your generator accepts, a start and end frame, a start frame only, reference images or text only, and the pack is trimmed to fit. When you bring a clip back, the studio checks that it begins and ends on your pictures.

An honest note about videoMonofilm does not generate video. Meta has no video model, so the studio prepares everything for the generator of your choice and checks what comes back.

For humans and for agents

The same studio, open to your AI agent.

Monofilm is also an MCP server. An agent can read a production, propose a change, or work a step, under the same rules as you: it proposes, a person approves, and every version is kept.

Built on Cloudflare Workers, D1 and R2 with plain vanilla JavaScript and no build step. The studio you click and the server your agent calls share one history.
