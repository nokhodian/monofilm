## Controls
- One flowing paragraph under 200 words; sizes divisible by 32; frames 8k+1; image and keyframe conditioning at target frames; video extension; seed saved to recreate. [pm-005]
- The repo is legacy; LTX-2/2.3/2.5 continue. [pm-005] [pm-026]

## Prompt dialect
- Present tense, join actions with as/then/while; state perspective, movement, speed, lens; describe the image exactly for image-to-video; sounds go in prose. [pm-026]
- Cinematic single-subject scenes with clear camera language work best (LTX-2.5 guide, search snippet). [pm-031]

## Known weaknesses
- Flicker, moire and exposure shifts; emotion labels. [pm-026]

## Consistency tools
- Keyframe list with per-item strength. [pm-005]

## Cost and limits
- Open weights; guidance 3-3.5, 40+ steps for quality. [pm-005]
