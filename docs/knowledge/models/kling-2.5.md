## Controls
- Text, image, start and end frames, motion brush, 5 or 10 s. [pm-008]
- Image-to-video start frame at least 300x300. [pm-019]

## Prompt dialect
- Six layers: subject, action, environment, camera, light, technical; 50-100 words. [pm-008]
- Subject, movement, scene, optional camera/light/atmosphere. [pm-019]
- One hero subject and one action; name the camera move exactly. [pm-028]
- Negative terms: morphing, distorted hands, flicker, jitter. [pm-008]

## Known weaknesses
- Lip-sync and dialogue weak; stiff static people; identity drift when appearance is over-described; ungrounded objects. [pm-008]
- No official Kling guide was reached; these are third-party. [pm-008]

## Consistency tools
- Start and end frames; keep appearance wording short to avoid drift. [pm-008]

## Cost and limits
- Test at 5 s before 10 s. [pm-008]
