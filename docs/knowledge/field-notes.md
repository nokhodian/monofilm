# Field notes

What we learned by running Monofilm ourselves (2026-11-05 to 2026-11-09), with the MiniMax H3 model on a local GPU and Meta's Muse Image and Muse Spark in the studio. These are our own observations, not sources: nothing here is cited as evidence in `practices/`. Each note says what happened, the cause we found, what we changed and where it lives. Add new notes at the end, newest last.

| # | What happened | Cause | What we did |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A cartoon penguin came out as a photographed man holding a toy; every later picture and clip followed it | The face prompt said "portrait photograph of one person" for every character | Prompts follow the film's medium and the kind of being (`mediumOf`, `beingOf`, `src/wizard.js` makeFace) |
| 2 | A bunny changed clothes inside one clip (pink scarf, blue overalls, pink scarf) | The words described blue overalls while the start picture showed something else; the generator flipped between them | With a start picture the words never describe a character again (`composeClip`, `film.pictures`) |
| 3 | Bubbles, a plant, a human hand and a bottle appeared in clips | The prompt named them: a generic physics line mentioned bubbles, water and hands, and the scene listed props that were not in view yet | Name only what is in view (`propsInClip`); never name what is hidden or not there yet; the physics line names no substance |
| 4 | One seed became several, or vanished | The plan had a tiny seed picked up and dropped on screen, which no generator shows | A tiny object is never handled on screen: it lies in place and the large action around it is shown |
| 5 | A plant appeared in the soil before the seed was planted | Pictures were drawn from action sentences, so an end picture could show the middle of the action | Scenes are written as states (one frozen picture per clip boundary) with fixed prop looks and counts; a continuity review corrects skipped stages |
| 6 | The scene reshaped between clips | Each end picture was drawn separately from the start picture | An end picture is drawn from the picture before it |
| 7 | Roll camera stopped with "model not found" once, and with a content-filter refusal on an innocent prompt | Meta's API is flaky for a moment; the "never show" list contained the word "guns" | Retry a one-off "model not found"; keep weapon words out of image prompts; retry a refusal without the clause, then with shortened sentences |
| 8 | A film got two sets of scenes | The Roll camera lease (150 s) was shorter than the scene draft (two model calls), so a second runner started | The lease outlasts the longest step; the draft checks for existing scenes inside every try |
| 9 | Retries stopped after one try | The attempt counter included takes made before the scene was changed | Takes older than the scene's last change do not count |
| 10 | A picture judge said "yes" to a bear drawn for a snail | A judge that is told what to expect agrees; fine details and kinds of animals are weak points | The kind of every named character is compared in code, after a blind description; a failing picture gets a second look |
| 11 | Scene pictures ignored the character references for many rounds | A comment I added on the same line as a loop swallowed the loop (a `//` comment followed by code on one line) | Fixed; a test asserts the reference reaches the image model. Never put code after a `//` comment |
| 12 | Snail and beetle were drawn as two bears | The names Moss and Pip are common words and pull an image model toward moss and a seed | Image prompts never use a character's name; they use its kind and look (`expandNames`, `plainDesc`) |

| 17 | A vision judge did not see a character change size | A judge is poor at proportions | SAM measures each character's box in the new and the previous picture; below 65% or above 150% of the previous size is a fault (`sizeProblem`). | 18 | A snail was drawn rearing up in the first picture and lying flat in the later ones | The first state did not name a posture | Every state names each character's posture |
| 19 | An empty place picture came out with little clay animals on it | A handmade clay look invites figurines | The place prompt asks for nothing living or figure-like |
| 20 | A snail shrank by 10–30% from one picture of a scene to the next and against the beetle; scale differed across scene cuts | Each picture was only compared with the one before it (small steps add up), and the first picture of a scene was held to the previous scene only loosely (65%) | Every picture is held to the previous one and to the scene's first picture (78–130%), and the size of one character relative to another is held too (80–125%); the static camera clause also says every character keeps its size and the background stays fixed (`sizeProblem`, `originUri` in `makeFrame`) |
| 21 | A character was still the wrong size after two redraws | An image model redraws the whole picture and ignores a size correction | A local repair: SAM mask, cut out, rescale about the ground point, fill, composite; measured 80% to 99% (enlarge) and 119% to 99% (shrink) on real pictures (`src/rescale.js`). | 22 | The measured size of a character jumped around, so checks passed and failed at random and a repair enlarged the wrong thing | SAM asked for "snail" also finds the beetle, and in some pictures with more confidence (0.93 against 0.91); the most confident box was taken. Not built yet: one continuous music bed under the film, brightness matching at a join, an early start of the next scene's sound |

| 25 | A take pushed in or jumped closer though its prompt said static camera | The generator reshapes the scene whatever the prompt says | The judge names the frames; the take is good until just before them (`jumpUntil`), and assembly cuts there |
| 26 | A character looked as if it shrank in the first half second of a clip, and later grew or shrank slowly without anything explaining it | The generator draws the first 0.5 to 0.7 s of a clip with the character 15 to 20% larger than the rest (in 6 of 8 takes: 0.59 then 0.48), and the old limits (88% to 114%) let slow drifts of 20% pass | The settling frames are measured (`settleOf`) and cut away in the assembly; the size is read from the frames after it; the limits are now 92% to 108% (80% to 125% for a character that really walks toward or away), 90% to 112% for a pair (`public/js/pack.js`) |
| 27 | A jump at a scene change: the picture went black for two frames and back | A dip through black was made as an overlap of 0.43 s because the next clip was only 1.3 s long, so it flashed | A dip is a fade out then a fade in, one after the other, with no overlap and no picture lost (`assemble-film.mjs`, `dip`) |
| 28 | Two different arrangements dissolved into each other and showed a ghost; the character at the end of one clip and at the start of the next were at different sizes | A clip that continues another starts from the planned picture, but the clip before was cut early, so its last shown frame is somewhere else | A clip that continues another starts from the frame where the clip before is cut (`cut_start` in `gen_clips.py`); a join with a large difference goes through black; the plan prints the size change at each join |
| 29 | The judge gave different answers about the same take | One vision judge call per review | Asked twice; a camera jump counts only when both answers see it. `scripts/measure-film.mjs` measures the size of every character in the finished film every half second |
| 30 | A small jump or dropped frame at joins and a judder in the motion | The clips are 24 fps but move at 12: every second frame is a near copy, and where the pairing shifts (a join, a trimmed start) the step is uneven; at a matched join the first frame of the next clip repeats the last of the one before, and lengths that were not whole frames repeated or lost a frame | `cadence` finds a clip whose steps are uneven and `smoothClip` rebuilds it at a true 24 fps by motion-compensated interpolation; the first frame of a matched next clip is skipped; every length is a whole number of frames (`assemble-film.mjs`). | 31 | A small jump at a join inside a scene that no pacing or matching fix removed | H3 redraws fine detail every frame (skin spots, shell glaze, eyestalk shape, a few pixels of position), and two takes never boil the same way, so any join between two takes shows the pattern changing; stabilising the picture did not help (the sky strip changed by 0.79 and 0.85 before and after) and a made-up in-between frame ghosts when a head turns | Clips are shown on twos (every real frame twice, evenly) by default; a join of a clip that continues another is a cut to a closer framing (14%, focus left of centre) when the two frames differ, so it reads as a new camera angle, and a 4 frame dissolve when they hardly differ (`joinKind`, `cutIn` in `assemble-film.mjs`) |

## MiniMax H3 (local, 8-step turbo): what we saw

- Poured water is drawn as a flat blue disc or ring; the words "bubbles" make bubbles appear.
- Cuts and framing jumps appear when a prompt lists several shots; a "slow push-in" sometimes pushes in then jumps back wide.
- The clip often reaches its end picture early and then stands still for 1 to 4 seconds. The assembly script trims it.
- A clip of 6 to 8 seconds is the useful length; a second take is needed for about half the clips; walking is weak, gliding and turning are fine.
- No motion-strength control exists in our ComfyUI graph (`MiniMaxH3ImageToVideo` takes only prompt, size, length and the two pictures). The sigma shift is the only nearby setting: shifts of 12, 20 and 32 gave the same motion (mean frame difference 0.88, 0.84, 0.82; hold 1.2 s each, same seed). Steer motion with the prompt and trim the frozen tail.
- Voices do not persist between clips; a silent clip still sometimes gets speech unless the prompt describes quiet room tone and closed mouths.
